Privacy Policy

Atlas Lake · last updated 15 October 2026

Who this covers

Atlas Lake is a private, single-user application. The only person who authorises it is its operator, using their own Google account. There are no other users, no accounts and no sign-up.

What Google data it accesses

The application requests the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive so that it can:

  • create and upload files into one folder of the operator's own Drive;
  • read those files back when running a query;
  • list that folder's contents to verify an upload completed intact.

It writes only to the folder the operator nominates. It does not open, modify, move or delete anything else in the Drive.

What it does not do

  • No Google data is sent to any third party.
  • No Google data is used for advertising, profiling or training any model.
  • No analytics, telemetry or tracking of any kind is collected.
  • Nothing is shared, published or made publicly accessible.

Credentials

The OAuth refresh token is held in encrypted secret storage and is used only to obtain short-lived access tokens for the Drive API. It is never written to a log, a repository or a file in the dataset. It can be revoked at any time from the operator's Google account permissions page, which immediately ends all access.

Retention

Files the application uploads remain in the operator's own Drive until the operator deletes them. No copy of any Google data is retained anywhere else.
